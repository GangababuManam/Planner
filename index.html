Threat Exposure Management

You Don't Have a Vulnerability Problem.
You Have an Exposure Problem.

Reduce Exposure. Build Resilience.

Enterprises already know they have vulnerabilities—often thousands or millions of findings across increasingly complex environments.

The real challenge is understanding which weaknesses can become meaningful business risk, how attackers could use them, and what security teams should do first.

Move from Findings to Exposure Intelligence

ANTRIQ brings vulnerability, asset, threat, identity, attack-path and business context together to help security teams move from managing findings to continuously managing exposure.

Discover → Contextualise → Prioritise → Remediate → Validate

Discover the Real Attack Surface

Continuously understand the assets, vulnerabilities, misconfigurations and security weaknesses that contribute to enterprise exposure.

  • Cloud and infrastructure
  • Applications and APIs
  • Endpoints and workloads
  • Identity and privilege
  • External attack surface
  • Security controls and configuration
  • AI infrastructure and intelligent systems
  • Third-party and technology dependencies
Context Changes Everything

A critical vulnerability does not automatically equal critical business risk. ANTRIQ enriches findings with the context required to understand actual exposure.

Vulnerability severity+Exploitability+Active threat intelligence+Asset criticality+Identity and privilege+Attack paths+Existing security controls+Business impact=Exposure Intelligence
100,000 Findings Don't Need 100,000 Actions.

The objective is not to make every finding look urgent. It is to identify the small number of actions that materially reduce risk.

100,000Security Findings→2,500Relevant Exposures→180Material Risks→15Priority Actions

The numbers above are illustrative website storytelling, not performance claims. They should be presented visually as an example of the prioritisation concept.

Prioritise What Attackers Can Actually Use

Traditional vulnerability management often asks, “How severe is this vulnerability?” Intelligent exposure management asks a broader question: “Can it be exploited, what can an attacker reach, what business capability is at risk, and what should we do now?”

Severity → ContextFindings → ExposureVolume → PriorityReactive patching → Risk reduction
From Dashboards to Decisions

Security teams do not need another dashboard telling them everything is critical. They need intelligence that improves decisions.

  • What is our current exposure?
  • Where are our highest-risk attack paths?
  • Which vulnerabilities are realistically exploitable?
  • Which assets and identities matter most?
  • What should we remediate first?
  • Which action will reduce the most risk?
  • Did remediation actually reduce exposure?
Reduce Exposure.

The objective is not to fix every vulnerability. It is to continuously reduce the exposure that matters—using intelligence to focus people, time and investment where they create the greatest security outcome.
